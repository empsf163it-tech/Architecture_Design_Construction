SITE CONTEXT

Plot-to-Home Planning

A home should be born from its topography, solar path, and municipal setbacks rather than dropped arbitrarily on the dirt.

Solar Path & Overhang Engineering

Position primary living glazing toward true solar south. Size roof overhangs to block high summer sun while allowing low winter rays.

Topographical Contours & Drainage

Work with existing contours to eliminate expensive retaining walls and maintain natural runoff away from foundation footings.

Prevailing Wind Vectors

Align opposing operable windows along the axis of summer prevailing breezes to induce natural passive stack-effect cooling.

Setback Boundaries & Easements

Identify front, side, and rear zoning setbacks, utility right-of-ways, and protected root zones before freezing the building envelope footprint.
