Our Point of View

Four core principles that guide every article, plan review, and recommendation we publish.

PRINCIPLE 01

Durability Over Novelty

We design for 50-year structural lifespans rather than fleeting 2-year social media fads. Timeless assemblies outlive trends.

Pillar 0150-Yr Life
PRINCIPLE 02

Material Honesty

Wood should celebrate its grain, stone should show its veining, and concrete should stand raw. We reject plastic faux veneers.

Pillar 02Honesty
PRINCIPLE 03

Envelope & Energy Literacy

Superior insulation, continuous air barriers, and solar orientation save far more money over 30 years than smart gadgets.

Pillar 03Passive
PRINCIPLE 04

A thoughtfully planned 1,800-square-foot home with efficient flow lives far better than a hollow, echoing 4,500-square-foot mansion.
