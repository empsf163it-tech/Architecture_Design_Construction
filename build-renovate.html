BUILDING SCIENCE

What Happens Behind the Walls

A high-performance exterior wall is a multi-layer climate machine designed for airtightness, vapor diffusion, and acoustic isolation.

1. Ventilated Cladding & Rainscreen Air Gap

A 3/8-inch ventilated air gap behind exterior siding allows wind-driven rain to drain and dry freely, permanently preventing rot.

2. Continuous Exterior Polyiso Insulation (R-10)

Rigid foam exterior insulation breaks thermal bridging across wood framing studs, cutting heating conduction by 40%.

3. 2x6 Framing Studs with Mineral Basalt Rockwool (R-23)

Dense mineral wool batts deliver high thermal resistance and natural non-combustible fire protection inside stud bays.

4. Intelligent Variable Vapor Retarder Membrane

Smart membrane adjusts permeability: blocks indoor winter humidity from entering studs while allowing summer drying.

5. PEX-A Expansion Plumbing Manifold

Dedicated home-run plumbing lines eliminate concealed in-wall tee fittings, eliminating the primary cause of hidden leaks.

6. Resilient Channel with Type-X Drywall

Acoustic decoupling clips isolate interior drywall from framing, stopping low-frequency vibration and conversation transfer.

CONTRACT & RISK MANAGEMENT

Working With Contractors

A legally sound agreement protects both homeowner and builder. Understand bidding structures and critical contract clauses.

FIELD-VERIFIED CONTRACTING PROTOCOLS

1. Fixed-Price (Stipulated Sum)

Best for fully completed architectural CAD drawings. Contractor assumes labor and material risk; any change requires written owner sign-off.

2. Cost-Plus with Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP)

Best for complex vintage remodels. Homeowner sees actual invoices with a guaranteed financial ceiling protecting the overall budget.

3. Mandatory Contract Safeguards

Require progressive lien waivers, a 10% retainage holdback until 100% punch-list completion, and direct underwriter Certificate of Insurance ($2M liability).
