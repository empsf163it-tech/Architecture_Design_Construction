ERGONOMIC ANATOMY

Room Design Breakdowns

An architectural breakdown of clearances, lighting hierarchies, and acoustics inside a master living and dining pavilion.

01

36-Inch Minimum Circulation Corridors

Ensuring clear pathways around seating islands and dining tables prevents the room from feeling cramped during gatherings.

02

Three-Tier Lighting Hierarchy

Combining concealed 2700K cove lighting (ambient), table reading lamps (task), and low art sconces (accent) on separate dimmers.

03

Acoustic Ceiling Absorption Baffles

Slatted timber ceiling panels with mineral wool backing absorb flutter echo from large floor-to-ceiling glass walls.

04

Cross-Ventilation Window Axis

Positioning operable awning windows across from sliding patio doors creates natural passive thermal relief during warm summers.
